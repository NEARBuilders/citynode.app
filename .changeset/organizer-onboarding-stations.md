Members of a team with the events area can read, create and edit their community's events, import Luma events and choose which imports to show, as owners and admins can. They can start an onboarding station from the event in My community, and owners, admins and events-team members can start one from Organization → Onboard by picking an upcoming event. Members without the events area still can't manage events or start a station. Only owners and admins can disconnect a community's Luma calendar, since that unpublishes every imported event.
