Someone whose only organization is personal now sees **Start a community** in the sidebar and as Home's first step. Personal organizations are hidden from the community switcher, `/orgs` and Home's counts, using one shared check. A member who signs in again with no active organization and one community has it opened for them. A member whose personal organization is active is asked to **Choose a community**, and My community shows the same choice instead of **No community yet**. Pending or rejected requests don't count as a community, so those users still see **Start a community**. Loading or failed organization reads no longer flash **Start a community**; Home offers a retry.
