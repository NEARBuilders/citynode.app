The Team stake card no longer offers a new unstake while earlier rewards are unlocking or an unstake or withdraw proposal for the pool (including the `unstake_all`, `withdraw_all` and `withdraw_all_from_staking_pool` variants) is open in the DAO, labels the balance by what is staked or unstaked underneath an open proposal, checks every in-progress or failed proposal still inside the DAO's voting period by chain time, re-reads the pool after every proposals read and the whole chain state before proposing and again once Trezu connects, right before signing, keeps actions off while any read is in flight, pauses actions when the pool, the chain's time or the DAO's policy, proposals or delegation balance can't be read, and shows only to community owners and admins, platform admins, members of a team with the Finance area, and accounts that can propose or approve in the DAO, including through Sputnik `Member` roles (shown paused when the DAO's policy can't be read). Its propose actions are enabled only for accounts the DAO lets propose; everyone else sees the state with a note pointing to Trezu. Members whose active team has the Finance area can now open My community → Overview (and see My community in the sidebar), while the other My community pages still need Node operations.
