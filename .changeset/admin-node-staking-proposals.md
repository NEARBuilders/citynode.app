Add a Node sequence tab to the admin community page. For a community with a DAO team treasury and its own staking pool, an admin can create the sputnik proposals the staking prototype ran: the team treasury stakes its own pool, registers with House of Stake and locks NEAR, the endowment treasury locks NEAR and stakes it with the team's pool, delegates its full veNEAR to the team treasury, the team treasury votes on a House of Stake proposal, and either treasury unstakes, withdraws and unwinds. The endowment account is saved on the community. Each action shows the current stake, lockup or voting power, and each step is blocked until the chain state allows it, marked done once it has landed, or linked to its pending proposal.
