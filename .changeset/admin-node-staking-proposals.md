Add a Node sequence tab to the admin community page. For a community with a DAO team treasury and a staking pool, an admin can create the sputnik proposals the staking prototype ran: the team treasury stakes its own pool, registers with House of Stake and locks NEAR, the endowment treasury locks NEAR and stakes it with the team's pool, delegates its full veNEAR to the team treasury, and the team treasury votes on a House of Stake proposal. Each step shows when it is already done on chain or has a pending proposal.
