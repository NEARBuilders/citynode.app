Keep My community Overview, Proposals and Stake open whatever team is active, since they are member destinations rather than area tools. The team switcher only lists teams with at least one recognized area and hides itself when none remain. A selected team with no recognized areas, or one the member left, now shows All areas straight away and is cleared from the session, with a retry toast if that fails. On Overview, event and finance tools follow the active team's areas, and work-area pages such as node settings still return to Home with an explanation when the active team does not cover them.
