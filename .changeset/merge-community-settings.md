Community settings is now the one place to edit a community: the published config editor (title, description, repository, custom UI bundle, DAO proposal status) moved there from the Organization page's Community and Homepage tabs. Organization keeps members, teams, invitations, onboarding and API keys, with a link to Community settings for owners and admins. Old `?tab=node-config` and `?tab=homepage` links redirect to the new section for owners and admins, and keep everyone else on Members. The public "Propose homepage change" button now shows only to owners and admins.
