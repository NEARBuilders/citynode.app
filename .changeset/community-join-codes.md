Every community keeps a join code. Communities that were created without one, including Chicago, India and New York City, receive a standing code the next time a platform admin, or that community's organizer, opens the map, a community page, or a join link. Later edits to community details keep that code. Signed-out visitors who choose Join are sent to sign in and brought back to the same community.
