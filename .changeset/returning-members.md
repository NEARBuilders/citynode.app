Organizers, owners and admins see their community's returning members on Overview: the share of people who checked in at two or more of its events, out of everyone who checked in at least once, all time. Check-ins are read per event across every node of the community. The stat says when it was read, shows **May be incomplete** when an event lists a full page of codes, says **No eligible check-ins yet** before anyone has checked in, and offers a retry when a read fails.
